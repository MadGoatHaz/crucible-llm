# Crucible LLM v0.1.2 — GPU Monitoring for Everyone

The biggest ask since the last update: "what about my AMD/Intel GPU?" Done.

**Multi-Vendor GPU Telemetry**
Crucible now auto-detects your GPU at startup and runs the matching backend — zero config:
- **NVIDIA** — full NVML telemetry: power, utilization, VRAM, temp, clocks, throttle reasons
- **AMD** — sysfs/hwmon: utilization, VRAM, temp, power, clocks (no ROCm stack needed)
- **Intel** — Level Zero Sysman for Arc (loaded at runtime, zero link dependency), sysfs fallback for i915/xe integrated

Launch the app, it finds your GPU, starts monitoring. No GPU? No panel. No N/A. Just clean.

**What You See in the TUI**
A live hardware panel during any benchmark run: watts, utilization, temp, VRAM, clocks, throttle flags, and — the fun one — **Joules/token** in real time.

**NVIDIA Now On by Default**
In v0.1.1, NVIDIA monitoring was behind a build flag — and in default builds it was silently off. That's fixed. A plain `cargo build` gives you full telemetry now.

**Energy Efficiency (Engine D)**
Joules-per-token is calculated live during any benchmark run. Compare your quantization levels, model sizes, or whole hardware setups on actual energy cost. Engine D auto-enables when a GPU is detected and stays off when it isn't, and your detected card shows right in Config: `D: Energy — [✓] RTX 4090`.
